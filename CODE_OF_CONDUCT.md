# Community conduct

Make this a useful, respectful place to collaborate. Welcome different backgrounds and experience levels, discuss technical disagreements with evidence, and give credit to contributors and upstream projects.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory behavior, personal attacks, doxxing and knowingly publishing another person's private information are unacceptable. Do not share credentials, private agent conversations, or customer information in issues and pull requests.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and restrict participation when needed. Raise sensitive concerns through the repository's private reporting route described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md); do not repeat private details in a public issue.

These expectations apply to this repository's issues, pull requests and other maintained collaboration surfaces. Automated agents are subject to the same expectations as human contributors.
